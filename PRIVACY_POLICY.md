# Privacy Policy for Tab Manager

**Last Updated:** January 9, 2025

## Overview

Tab Manager ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how the Extension handles data.

## Data Collection

**We do NOT collect any personal data.**

The Extension does not:
- Collect personal information
- Track your browsing history
- Send data to external servers
- Use analytics or tracking tools
- Store data on remote servers

## Data Processing

All data processing happens **locally in your browser**:

### What the Extension Accesses:
- **Current tabs information** - Tab titles and URLs from your current browser window
- **Saved sessions** - Your named tab sessions stored locally
- **Your preferences** - Language settings and other preferences

### Where Data is Stored:
- **Local browser storage only** - All sessions and preferences are stored locally using Chrome's storage API
- **No cloud storage** - Nothing is uploaded or synchronized to external servers
- **No account required** - All data stays on your device

## Permissions Explanation

The Extension requests the following permissions:

### tabs
- **Purpose:** To read current tab information and create new tabs when restoring sessions
- **Usage:** Activated when you save or restore tab sessions
- **Data retention:** Tab information is only stored in sessions you explicitly save

### storage
- **Purpose:** To save your tab sessions and preferences locally
- **Usage:** Stores session data and settings in your browser's local storage
- **Data retention:** Data remains until you delete sessions or uninstall the extension

## Third-Party Services

The Extension does **NOT** use any third-party services, including:
- No analytics (e.g., Google Analytics)
- No tracking pixels
- No advertising networks
- No external APIs
- No cloud services

## Data Sharing

We do **NOT** share any data with third parties because we do not collect any data.

## Data Security

Since all processing happens locally in your browser:
- Your data never leaves your device
- No transmission over the internet
- No risk of data breaches from our servers (we don't have any)
- Complete privacy for your browsing sessions

## Children's Privacy

The Extension does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we don't collect any personal information from anyone, this policy applies to all users regardless of age.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

## Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no data to:
- Access
- Modify
- Delete
- Export

However, you can:
- Delete individual sessions within the extension
- Uninstall the extension at any time
- Clear your browser's local storage to remove all saved sessions
